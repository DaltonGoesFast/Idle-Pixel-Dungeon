# Balance

All numbers live in `data/*.json`; the tables below are generated by `python3 tools/gen_docs.py`.

## Core math (deterministic, no RNG in combat)

- **Collapsing SPD ranges:** SPD rolls `NormalIntRange(min, max)`; we use `floor((min + max) / 2)`.
  - Weapons: SPD `min = tier`, `max = 5 * (tier + 1)` -> Worn Shortsword (t1) 1-10 -> **5**.
  - Armor: SPD `DRMax = 2 * tier` -> Cloth (t1) 0-2 -> **1** flat DR.
  - Strength requirement: SPD `8 + 2 * tier - floor((sqrt(8*lvl + 1) - 1) / 2)` (t1 10, t2 12, t3 14 at +0; +1 and +3 each lower it by 1; Greataxe uses tier 6 = 20) (DECISIONS #207).
- **Physical damage taken** = `max(0, dmg - DR)`. Then Barrier absorbs first, then HP.
- **Chip rule:** a hit fully blocked by DR still removes 1 Barrier.
- **Barrier** (SPD-like, DECISIONS #147, #218, #221) = class base (5 Warrior, 3 others) + heirloom (Broken Seal +2) + armor barrier (`barrier` + `barrier_per_lvl`) + Ring of Tenacity + talents, **capped at `HT / 2`**. Refreshes at the start of every fight (0 in darkness). When no enemy is adjacent to the hero, Barrier **decays by 1 per turn** after the first 3 ticks of the fight. Potion of Shielding grants `max(10, 30% HT)` then clamps to `HT / 2`. Camp compare shows armor Barrier and notes when the cap eats the increase.
- **Surprise attack** (SPD-style: the foe cannot see you, or just lost sight after a door closed / you circled tall grass): `(dmg + surprise bonus) * 1.5` (Assassin `* 2.5`), then subtract enemy DR (minimum 1). Stealth weapons (Dagger, Dirk, Assassin's Blade) and Assassin pierce 50% of DR. No knockback from surprise — displacement is Shove, Slam, Blast Wave, Terror. Snakes slip every non-surprise hit (accurate weapons land some, below). Dagger-family surprise bonus: SPD rolls between `min + round((max-min) * pct)` and max (Dagger 75%, Dirk 67%, Assassin's Blade 50%), so the hit is the average of that roll. The Flail and a weapon you lack the Str for can't surprise attack. Warrior knockback into a wall/unit = 1 dmg; into a chasm = instant kill (loot halved, recovered when you descend).
- **Sight:** walls, tall grass, and closed doors block vision (open doors do not). Wands and the Spirit Bow need a clear view; the bow cannot fire at an adjacent tile.
- **Strength shortfall:** under-strength weapon damage is divided by `(1 + missing Str)` and weapon block is zeroed; under-strength armor DR is divided the same way and armor Barrier is 0 (DECISIONS #218, #220).
- **Weapons (SPD v4, DECISIONS #207, #220):** dmg = floor((min+max)/2) with min `tier + lvl * max(1, tier - 2)` and each weapon's own max and max-per-level (table below). Delay (`DLY`) = ticks per swing; reach 2-3 (Spear, Glaive, Whip) hits foes that far away along a free path of at most that many steps with line of sight (walls and bodies block, like SPD's `canReach`). Block `0-N` -> DR `ceil(N/2)`. Accuracy has no SPD roll here: an inaccurate weapon (ACC < 1) misses `1 - ACC` of its swings at alert foes on a fixed rhythm; an accurate one (ACC > 1) lands `ACC - 1` of its swings on dodging foes (snakes).
- **Upgrades:** weapon max per level from its formula (above); armor uses `dr_per_lvl` / `barrier_per_lvl` (#218); capped by Blacksmith level.
- **Traps:** Worn Dart deals `3 + floor/2` physical damage (DR applies); other traps keep their flat numbers.
- **Turn clock:** SPD-style. Each actor has a `next` time; hero wins ties. Normal = 1.0, fast (crab) = 0.5, slow weapons (Spear, Glaive) = 1.5, fast ones 0.8 (Scimitar) or 0.5 (Studded Gloves, Sai, Stone Gauntlet) ticks. The Mace is normal speed, as in SPD.
- **Levels:** SPD `maxExp = 5 + 5 * level`; +5 HT per level (SPD).
- **Telegraphs:** Gnoll Scout throws (dmg + 2) at your tile from range 2-3, resolving 1 tick later. Goo pumps up every 4th action (every 3rd below half HP): all 8 surrounding tiles take `dmg * 3` one tick later. Don't fight it in a corner. Prison: Skeleton bone burst (3x3, 1 tick after death), DM-100 zap (range 2-4), Tengu shuriken (1 tick) and bombs (3x3, 2 ticks, hurt mobs too).
- **Traps:** one-shot. Worn Dart 3 dmg (DR applies), Gripping 2 dmg + lose a turn, Teleportation (far side of the room), Poison Dart 1 + 1/turn for 4 turns (ignores DR/Barrier). Search = 2 ticks, radius 2 (Rogue/Huntress 3).
- **Rogue Cloak:** SPD Cloak of Shadows at +0 (DECISIONS #121, #206): 3 charges for the whole expedition, not per fight. A fresh expedition starts full; each cleared fight gives 1 back (cap 3); Mystical Meal (Rogue tier 2) gives 1 more when a ration is eaten; a Scroll of Recharging adds 1 at the start of the next fight, never above 3, kept until spent (#211). Auto Battle and Hint spend charges only on a boss, an emergency (HP <= 40% or a projected loss >= HP), a full cloak, a stalled snake fight, or, keeping the last charge in reserve, an elite, 3+ foes or a projected loss >= 50% of HP (#211). No subclass or talent raises the cap. Plus keen eyes: auto-reveals adjacent hidden traps. 1 tick to activate, hidden for 2 more ticks: enemies stop chasing/attacking/throwing; the next attack is a Surprise (dagger-family bonus) and breaks the cloak.
- **Huntress Spirit Bow:** unlimited arrows at range 2-6 with line of sight (no point-blank), dmg = floor(avg of SPD (1 + lvl/5)-(6 + lvl/2.5)), DR applies, Surprise when the foe lost sight. Studded Gloves melee: 3 dmg at 0.5 ticks.
- **Mage staff bolt:** 2 charges (+1 at Lv7, Lv14...), +1 per 4 ticks, ignores DR.

## Delve
- Torch drains 0.4 per 1% of floor progress (a full Sewers run = 200 torch = the starting 100 + 2 torches), satiety 0.4. A torch / ration is auto-used under 20 (restores 60 / 70).
- Pack cap (DECISIONS #144): rations and torches per expedition = 3 / 6 / 10 at Guild Lv1 / 2 / 3. A full Sewers run needs about 2 torches beyond the starting 100, so Lv1 covers the Sewers; deep multi-biome runs need Guild Lv3.
- Torch at 0 = **darkness**: fights start with 0 Barrier. Satiety at 0 = -1 HP per 5% (never below 1).
- Passive gathering: biome-scaled gold every 8% (Sewers 1 … Halls 5), dew on a biome interval (Sewers ~1/floor ≈ 5/delve; denser later).
- 4 events per floor at ~22/45/68/90% (+-2, seeded). Event 1 is always a fight; the rest roll combat 5 : door 2 : special 2. Floor 5's last event is Goo, floor 10's is Tengu. A cleared floor can be searched once for hidden doors: -10 torch, 50% (Rogue 100%) secret cache.
- A Sewers+Prison run needs ~400 torch: start 100 + 5 torches.
- Descending heals 20% of max HP. Potions of Strength at fixed milestones: floor 2 @ 50% and floor 4 @ 50%.

## Patrols (DECISIONS #130, #188, #222)
- **Yield** per material = biome rate/h x hours worked x efficiency x min(1.50, 1 + gear bonus + perk %). Hours worked = real hours (capped at the Guild's 2/4/8h, plus a Timekeeper's Hourglass) x (1 + Ring of Haste %).
- **Efficiency** = clamp((power rating + patrol power perks) / (biome Str gate + 2), 0.25, 1.0).
- **Gear bonus** = 0.08 x sqrt(total gear level) (+8% at 1, +18% at 5, +25% at 10, +32% at 16), added with perk % and capped at +50% total. Gold gets no gear share; Wealth / Armband / Haste / Hourglass still raise gold. Fee = 1 hour of efficiency-scaled gold.
- **Halls rates** (geared, 8h, after fee): about a quarter of one Tier 5 weapon forged and taken to +3 (`45g / 4 ore / 1.4 dark gold / 0.7 resin` per hour).
- **Perks** (table in CONTENT.md) come from the worn ring, artifact and staff wand and scale with item level. Flat finds (Sandals, Regrowth) are per hour x efficiency; timed drops (Chalice potions, Horn rations) are per hours worked.
- **Curios**: one per `patrol_curio_every_hours` worked (x (1 + curio perk %)), Might/Tenacity unknown +0 by default, alternating; Ring of Wealth opens every ring, Talisman of Foresight identifies them and makes some +1.
- **Fee** = half a full-length patrol's base gold for the biome (Ring of Elements cuts it, max 50%).
- Everything is locked in when the patrol starts.
- Sim (PATROL_SELF=1: the delving hero runs one full patrol of its deepest cleared biome between every expedition, 4 classes x 2 seeds x 40 runs): per-patrol ore +47%, dark gold +42%, resin +41%, gross gold +19%, net gold after fees +31% vs before #188; median end gold 2131 -> 2949, median end resin 369 -> 530. The standard campaign sim never patrols (other heroes never clear a biome), so it is unchanged.

## Why the crab changed (v0.2.0)
SPD's crab (dmg 1-7 -> 4, DR 0-4 -> 2, speed x2) dealt ~6 damage per tick to a 20 HP Warrior while needing 5 hits to kill. In SPD the hero's evasion and the crab's misses make that survivable; with 100% accuracy it was not. Crab is now dmg 2 / DR 1 (still double speed, so it strips Barrier fast - the GDD "swarm" role). Goo went HT 100 -> 50 and DR 0-2 -> 0 for a similar reason (low-damage classes needed 25+ hits).

## Verification
`tests/run_tests.gd` plays every encounter set in every room with `CombatBot` (sensible tactics, no hindsight): the starting Warrior must win all of them, the Mage >= 90%, and a Lv3/Lv4 Warrior with 2 potions must beat Goo. `[fullrun]` additionally plays 10 seeded full expeditions per class (2 rations, 2 torches, 2 potions); each class must clear >= 5/10. Results at v0.5.0: Warrior 9/10, Mage 9/10, Rogue 9/10, Huntress 10/10 (v0.4.0 was 9/7/6/8; see DECISIONS #40-41).

`[prison]`: a Lv5 Warrior (Str 12, Shortsword+1, Leather+1) must win >= 95% of Prison encounter x room combinations (currently 99/99, lowest end HP 34/40) and beat Tengu with 2 potions. `[deeprun]`: Lv1 hero with forged tier-2 gear plays Sewers + Prison in one expedition (6 rations, 8 torches, 4 potions): Warrior 10/10, Mage 6/10 (3/10 before v0.8.0), Rogue 6/10, Huntress 10/10 - every death is at Tengu.

## Prison readiness
GDD target: 12-13 Str, tier 2-3 gear. Two Sewers Strength potions take a fresh hero from 10 to 12 Str by floor 4, so a hero who forged a Shortsword/Leather Armor before the run arrives at the Prison able to use them.
